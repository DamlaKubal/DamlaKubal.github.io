About Me

I am a final-year Biochemistry student at Imperial College London with research experience in molecular biology, cloning, bioinformatics and computational analysis. My main academic interests are in neuroscience, particularly the molecular mechanisms underlying neurodegeneration, neuronal survival and repair. I am interested in combining experimental and computational approaches to investigate these processes and intend to pursue master's-level research training before doctoral study.
